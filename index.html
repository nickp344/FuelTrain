What is RED-S?

Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (RED-S) can develop when someone repeatedly takes in too little energy to support both training and the body’s everyday needs. It can affect health, recovery and performance over time.

There is no single calorie number that can diagnose RED-S. A sports-medicine professional assesses several signs and symptoms. This app can’t diagnose it.

Learn more: IOC consensus · Australian Institute of Sport
